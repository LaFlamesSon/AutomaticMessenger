Privacy

Privacy, in plain language.

This policy describes the data CaughtUp uses to run its inbox and creator-opportunity workflows, the services that process it, and the choices available to users.

1. Scope and operator

This policy applies to the CaughtUp Chrome extension, website, and related backend services. CaughtUp is the service name used by its owner and operator. This policy is effective August 14, 2026.

3. How we use data

We use data only to provide, secure, maintain, and improve the CaughtUp features a user chooses to use. This includes classifying user-forwarded messages, preparing CaughtUp-stored replies, selecting media kits, displaying work, maintaining negotiation context, sending authorized replies, and matching eligible opportunities.

CaughtUp does not sell personal data. CaughtUp does not use Google, forwarded-email, or TikTok Shop data for targeted advertising. Product recommendations do not guarantee acceptance, sales, commissions, or earnings.

4. Infrastructure and service providers

CaughtUp uses Cloudflare Email Routing to receive user-configured forwarding and uses Supabase Edge Functions, Postgres, private Storage, authentication services, and Vault-backed configuration for its backend. For bounded email classification and drafting tasks, relevant forwarded content may be sent to the configured language-model provider. That provider is used as a processor for the requested task, not as an independent advertising service. CaughtUp erases the stored raw forwarded body after processing and retains the derived state needed to show and operate the workflow.

Google, Cloudflare, Supabase, the configured language-model provider, and TikTok process data under their own terms and privacy policies when their services are used.

5. Tokens and security

OAuth credentials and sensitive integration configuration are kept server-side under the established encrypted secret-storage design. The extension does not persist TikTok or Gmail refresh tokens in browser storage. Access to CaughtUp data is scoped to the owning account, and service-only database tables use row-level security.

No online service can promise absolute security. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards and review access boundaries as the product changes.

6. Retention and deletion

Account settings, media kits, processed-email summaries, CaughtUp drafts and edits, negotiation state, chat or style history, connection records, delivery state, and related product data are retained while the account remains active and as needed to provide the service. Raw forwarded message bodies are erased after processing. Operational logs may be retained for security, troubleshooting, and reliability. We do not keep personal data longer than reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Disabling a CaughtUp forwarding address stops CaughtUp from accepting new mail at that address, but the user must separately remove or disable the forwarding rule in Gmail. Revoking Google OAuth stops future authorized Gmail sends. Neither action automatically deletes records already stored by CaughtUp. A user may request deletion of the account and stored CaughtUp data. After a verified deletion request is completed, data is removed from active systems except where limited retention is reasonably necessary for security, legal compliance, fraud prevention, or backup recovery cycles.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on location, users may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, object to, or delete personal data. Users can disable CaughtUp intake, remove the forwarding rule in Gmail, revoke Google access through their Google Account, disconnect supported connections, and separately request deletion of CaughtUp-held data.

8. Children

CaughtUp is intended for creators who are legally able to authorize business-account connections and enter contracts. It is not directed to children under 13.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the product and its integrations change. The published policy will identify its effective date, and material changes will be communicated through an appropriate product or account notice.
