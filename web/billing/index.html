Return to CaughtUp to check your subscription.

Stripe sends you back here after checkout or billing management. Your subscription is confirmed securely by Stripe’s signed webhook—not by anything in this page’s URL.

Next step

Need help?

Email support@getcaughtup.io. Do not send card details, passwords, tokens, or message archives.
