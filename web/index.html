One place for the work hiding in your email.

CaughtUp is designed around reviewable assistance: it surfaces what matters, prepares a useful next step, and leaves consequential choices with you.

01

Inbox triage

Recent Gmail messages are classified so urgent work and action-needed conversations rise above low-priority noise.

02

Draft review

Open, edit, and confirm a bounded reply. Replace or remove the single suggested media kit before deciding whether to send.

03

Negotiation context

Commercial terms are highlighted in the thread timeline and always stay review-only—CaughtUp does not accept, reject, or counter for you.

04

Media-kit matching

Use your owned kits and matching rules to bring the most relevant context into a collaboration reply without guessing.

05

Creator opportunities

Rank configured affiliate products using creator-selected interests, audience context, listing evidence, and media-kit positioning.

06

Your communication style
