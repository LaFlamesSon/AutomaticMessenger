# TikTok Shop Security Questionnaire Answer Sheet

Prepared: August 10, 2026

This sheet maps each questionnaire item to the adopted CaughtUp policy evidence.
Historical answers remain owner attestations. Independent certification and DPO
status must not be inferred from a service provider.

## Security program

1. Published information security policy or program: Yes after the public
   Security page is deployed. Upload the combined Security and Privacy Program.

2. Network segregation and threat protection: Yes. Upload the Network and
   Endpoint Security Policy. CaughtUp uses managed service boundaries, Cloudflare
   edge protection, HTTPS, authenticated application endpoints, owner scoping,
   private storage, row-level security, and service-only roles.

3. Antivirus on company endpoints: Yes if the verified Windows endpoint is the
   only company endpoint. Microsoft Defender antivirus and real-time protection
   were verified enabled on August 10, 2026. Confirm any additional endpoints.

4. Daily security baseline: Yes only after the owner confirms MFA on every
   administrative account and confirms every company endpoint follows the
   adopted baseline. Upload the Network and Endpoint Security Policy.

5. Published access-control and least-privilege policy: Yes. Upload the Access
   Control Policy.

6. Published data-classification and encryption policy: Yes. Upload the Data
   Classification and Encryption Policy.

7. Published incident-response policy: Yes. Upload the Incident Response and
   Breach Notification Policy.

8. Vulnerability or threat-management procedure: Yes. Upload the Vulnerability
   and Threat Management Policy.

## Privacy and compliance

9. Reportable personal-data breach in the past three years: Owner attestation.
   Select No only if no such event occurred.

10. Regulatory, customer, or individual privacy complaint in the past three
    years: Owner attestation. Select No only if none was received.

11. Countries where data is stored or processed: United States for primary
    database and storage. Network requests, edge functions, and authorized
    providers may process data in other countries where they operate.

12. Internal personal-data protection policy: Yes. Upload the Personal Data
    Protection and Deletion Policy.

13. Assist sellers or TikTok Shop with access, correction, provision, or deletion
    requests: Yes. Use the privacy request channel support@getcaughtup.io.

14. Regularly updated privacy policy: Yes. Public URL:
    https://getcaughtup.io/privacy/

15. Appointed Data Protection Officer: No. The owner acts as Privacy Lead but is
    not represented as a formally appointed DPO.

16. Breach-notification process: Yes. Upload the Incident Response and Breach
    Notification Policy.

17. Delete customer data at the end of the relationship: Yes, subject to limited
    legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute, and provider backup-recovery
    exceptions. Upload the Personal Data Protection and Deletion Policy.

18. Independent industry certification: No. CaughtUp has not independently
    obtained ISO 27001, ISO 27701, SOC 2 Type 2, ePrivacy, or an equivalent
    certification.
